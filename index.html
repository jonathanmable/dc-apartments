How it works

  • Each building's own site. Listing sites were wrong on prices by $125 to $450 and on in-unit laundry, so only the building's own pages are read, as a visitor would.
  • One check a day. 8 am Eastern, or within an hour of the laptop waking. A building whose site doesn't answer keeps its last good reading and says when that was; its units are never marked gone by a failed read.
  • Floor plans read once. Closet area, walk-in and island are read from each floor-plan image by an AI model, so closet area is marked est. Checked against 40 floor plans measured by hand at another building: walk-in and island agreed on 39 and 40 of them, and closet area was within 30% on 31. The instructions were tuned on those same plans, so open the plan before relying on a number.
  • Deals. Specials change week to week. Each check records the deal as it stood that day, so history isn't rewritten when a deal changes.
  • What it costs. Nothing per use. Free hosting; floor plans are read on a flat-rate subscription.
  • Who built it. One person set the goals and made the calls. AI agents (Claude Code) built and run it.
